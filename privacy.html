Privacy Policy

xAgent · last updated 7 October 2026

Who uses the app

xAgent is a private, self-hosted bot. The only Google account that signs in to it is the bot's own dedicated account, controlled by the operator. The app does not ask other people to sign in with Google.

What Google data the app accesses

  • Google Drive (drive.file): only documents the app created itself — to publish notes as Google Docs and manage link sharing for them.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): events in the bot's own calendar — to list upcoming meetings and create meetings on request.
  • Gmail (gmail.readonly, gmail.compose): messages in the bot's own mailbox — to search and read them, prepare reply drafts, and send a draft only after the operator explicitly approves it.

How the data is used

Google data is used only to perform tasks the operator requests through Telegram. To perform such a task, relevant content may be processed by the AI model provider the bot runs on (OpenAI) solely to produce the response. Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train or improve AI models.

Storage and retention

Data is kept on the operator's private server, in a log of the bot's actions and conversations, for limited periods set in the bot's configuration, and is deleted automatically afterwards. The app does not delete mail or mark it as read.

Sharing

Google data is not shared with third parties, except the processing described above that is needed to perform a requested task, or where required by law.

Limited Use

xAgent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue in this site's repository.
